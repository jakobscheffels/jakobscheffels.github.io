About

I am a PhD student at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) and part of the Engineering Risk Analysis Group.

My research focuses on model order reduction methods for Bayesian inverse problems and uncertainty quantification in computational engineering systems.

Research Overview

Research

My research focuses on efficient uncertainty quantification for large-scale engineering systems.

I develop model order reduction techniques for computationally expensive Bayesian inverse problems, enabling scalable parameter estimation under uncertainty with limited observational data.

Current topics include reduced basis methods, surrogate modeling, and probabilistic inference for high-dimensional systems.
