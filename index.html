About

I am a PhD student at the Technical University Munich (TUM) and part of the Engineering Risk Analysis Group (ERA). I am passionate about Model Order Reduction for Bayesian inverse problems.

Research

My research focuses on the development of efficient uncertainty quantification in engineering systems. Model order reduction methods allow to construct cheap approximations of high-dimensional, computationally expensive models. I am developing methods for efficient estimation of uncertain parameters with limited data for Bayesian inverse problems.
